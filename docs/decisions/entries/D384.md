## D384: `main` gets an append-only ruleset first; the pull-request and checks rules wait for D300

- Status: amends D175
- Date: 2026-11-08 (the owner, on a decision card: "Block force-push and delete now")
- Decision: the ruleset for `main` (`.github/rulesets/main.json`, runbook 12; intended until the owner imports it, which `docs/state.md` tracks) holds no deletion, no force push and linear history, with no bypass actor. Squash-only merging and deleting head branches on merge are Settings to turn on (`docs/state.md` tracks them). D175's pull-request, resolved-threads and required-checks rules are not imported yet. They return, with `browser gate` (#551) in place of the `browser` check, once no workflow commits to `main`.
- Why: the engine's workflows (`run.yml`, `podcast.yml`, `audio.yml`, `config.yml`) push `state/*.json` straight to `main` with the workflow token. A pull-request rule would fail every one of those commits, and D175's bypass cannot exist: GitHub rejects the Actions app as a bypass actor on a personal repository ("invalid actor"). Blocking deletion and force pushes costs the engine nothing and removes the damage that cannot be undone.
- Context changed: D175 assumed the Actions app could bypass the ruleset; on a personal repository it cannot. D175's required `browser` check also never reports on a site PR, since the suites report as `browser / site suite` and `browser / player suite`.
- Rejected: a GitHub App the owner creates to push as the bypass actor (a new secret to rotate and four workflows to change, for commits D300 removes); waiting with `main` fully open until D300 ends; a person as a bypass actor (D175).
- Revisit if: D300 step 5 stops `state/` commits and no workflow pushes to `main` anymore, and the `browser gate` job is merged in `ci.yml` and seen reporting on a pull request (#551 or its replacement; the bare `browser` check never reports on a site PR). Then import the full ruleset (pull request with threads resolved, `check` and `browser gate` required) and decide whether the branch must be up to date at merge. Until then `docs/state.md` tracks the import.
