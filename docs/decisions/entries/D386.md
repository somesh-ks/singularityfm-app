## D386: A weekly that will not air, or did not, is a health fault, and a switched-off podcast says Off

- Status: amends D61; active
- Date: 2026-11-09 (the weekly of 8 Nov never aired: the Weekly switch was off from 4 Nov, an accidental tap, and nothing said so)
- Decision: health reads the effective schedule and the last weekly record. The week after one that aired, a weekly whose switch is off raises `weekly_off` on the day before and the day itself, and a weekly day that passed with no weekly run raises `weekly_missed` until the next weekly day, saying the cause (the switch, or no run). Settings' Daily and Weekly rows read "Off" when switched off, where they read their schedule.
- Why: a switched-off podcast is never due, so `run_overdue`, `slot_missed` and the Sunday run all stayed quiet, health was green, and the Settings list still read "Sundays at 8". The safeguards covered a run that misses its slot, not a slot that is off.
- Consequences: one fault opens the usual `health` issue and closes with the next green run; nothing is healed (a weekly started late would pay for models on a guess). A weekly that stays off for good is quiet after the one week, so a standing red does not hold the issue's healing. The record is the last weekly, not a history, so the check cannot say more than that week. A daily switched off is still never overdue; its row says Off.
- Revisit if: the owner turns the weekly off on purpose and the one red week is noise (add an "off until" date), or a second podcast kind gets a switch.
