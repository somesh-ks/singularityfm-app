## D388: A control's box never depends on its state, and a stylesheet holds no pixel length

- Status: active; amended by D391 (controls hug their words, no reserve)
- Date: 2026-11-10 (the owner saw the Play chip and filter chip change width as an icon or word appeared, then rejected a fixed 141.5px width: "avoid pixel values as hardcoded thing, as a rule")
- Decision: the library's DS-9 and DS-10 govern. A control reserves the widest words it can say (`reserve`, drawn hidden in the same grid cell), so a swap of icon, label or state never moves its box or a neighbour. A stylesheet holds a token, rem, em, ch or a calc() of them, never a measured pixel length; `design-check` runs `raw-px`, and `styles.test.ts` reads the library's `rawPixels`. Zero, the 1px hairline and a named drawing unit (`--art-unit` for the empty-state art) pass. Processing is the words and a shimmer, with no loading circle; only buffering during playback draws the spinner.
- Why: a width found by measuring one state's words goes stale with the next label, text size or mood, and a hardcoded size hides the cause of a jump instead of removing it.
- Consequences: the app took the library at 0.7.0 and dropped its page rules that pinned the Play button's width and spinner display. Seventeen old pixel lengths in the app's stylesheets became tokens, rem or the drawing unit; twenty page captures before and after are pixel identical. The hero Play chip is wider at rest (it holds "Processing").
- Revisit if: a control needs a width no word can reserve (an image or a number that grows without a bound), or Firefox draws the shimmer mask differently.
