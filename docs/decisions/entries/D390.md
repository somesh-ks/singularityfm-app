## D390: On a phone, the floating Play is the play mark alone

- Status: amends D59
- Date: 2026-11-11 (the owner, shown the floating Play with its words centred in a reserved width: "Play button to remain just an icon FAB like before")
- Decision: amends D59. The floating Play on an episode or an older briefing is a circle with the play mark and no words, at every scroll position; Create keeps its word and its fold. Its accessible name still says the hero chip's words after the verb ("Resume · 1 min left, the podcast"), written by the player. While narration makes the first file the mark shimmers, as a Play chip's waiting words do (D388), with no spinner.
- Why: the hero chip already says the length and the resume point above the fold; the floating button only has to be the way to play once that chip has scrolled away, and the words held for "Resume · 88 min left" left a wide pill with a gap in the middle.
- Consequences: the reserved width and the scroll fold are gone from Play, so it never changes size. The page reads less about where it was left from the button; the hero chip and the cards still say it.
- Revisit if: listeners press it expecting something other than playing this page's audio, or the shimmering mark is not read as working.
