## D391: Buttons hug their words; the hero's and each story's Play are the glyph alone

- Status: active; amends D388
- Date: 2026-11-13 (the owner, with a phone screenshot of a story's "0:22" in a wide pill: "in general buttons should hug text"; "the play button in the hero should be just the icon button, and the same in chapters")
- Decision: the library's DS-13 governs. A control is as wide as the words it says now; only its glyph slot is fixed, so a mark that swaps moves nothing. No page passes `reserve` (deprecated). The hero's Play and each story's draw PlayChip with no label: a circle as tall as the chip, beside the hero's Favorite and Share, its length and state in its name, which readerSync writes. The floating Play was already the glyph alone (D390). Selects hug the chosen option.
- Why: reserving the widest words read as a fixed width on a phone, the thing D388's own rule forbids. With no words on the Play buttons nothing is left to reserve.
