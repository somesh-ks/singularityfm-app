## D393: CI runs in parallel, and a red shard is read by cause

- Status: amends D148, D385
- Date: 2026-11-13 (the owner: "make the CI parallel. also make it comprehensive and hardened to avoid failures")
- Decision: `ci.yml` gets a `changes` job (the path filter, seconds long), and `check` and `browser` both start from it instead of `browser` waiting for `check`. `browser.yml` runs five shards at once: the player suite in 2 (by file, one worker) and the site suite in 3 (by test), each job capped at 15 minutes and stopped at 15 failed tests. A shard runs through `scripts/ciSuite.mjs`, which reads Playwright's JSON report: a failed test is `code` (no rerun); a run that never reached the tests (webServer timeout, SIGKILL, a network reset) or whose failures are all a dead server is `infra` and reruns once, said on the job page; a test that passed only on its retry is `flaky`, named and not failed; anything else is `unknown`. The pnpm and Chromium installs retry 3 times (`scripts/retry.sh`), the only steps that do. A `report` job sums the site shards into the one `browser-results` artifact the daily health check reads (D360). `check` and `browser gate` keep their names.
- Why: a site PR took 19 minutes (3 for `check`, then 15 for the site suite, 8 for the player in parallel) and the 30-minute cap cancelled a whole suite on one slow runner (2026-11-13). Retrying everything hides a regression; retrying nothing makes a dead runner look like a code fault. Sentry watches the running app, not CI, so the job page is the CI equivalent.
- Consequences: a PR starts 6 jobs (public repository, free), so about three PRs at once reach the 20-job limit and queue. A red `check` no longer stops the shards; they finish on free minutes. D148's one job for the private repository's minutes no longer holds here (the admin repository keeps its own CI).
- Revisit if: a shard passes 6 minutes (add one), the 20-job limit queues PRs, or `unknown` shows up more than once a week.
