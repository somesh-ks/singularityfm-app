## D394: Every state the reader tells is a strip, an empty screen, a toast or a field error

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13
- Decision: the strip is the one place for a one-line state, above everything including the open player (design system `--z-mini` + 1): offline, back online and the player's line, one at a time in that order, words in `lib/stripMessages.ts`. A recording with no audio replaces the player's cover and controls with an empty screen (Open Downloads, Try again); the strip has no button, and a missing file is asked for again twice by itself first. The player's inline red notice, its portal and its scroll-into-view are removed. Shared failure sentences move to `lib/failureCopy.ts`. A daily or monthly narration limit is no strip: it is a toast on the press, and the bar says "Daily limit reached". When the content store does not answer, a page drawn on request answers 503 with an empty screen (`pages/unavailable.astro`; Try again, Open Downloads; asked again on a gap, never saying offline) instead of the saved copy, which amends D282; Downloads and Saved still answer from it (`no-store`) as the way out, and the data files answer a plain 503.
- Why: during the Blob outage the player drew its own red block and a retry glyph on Play, a fifth way to say a state, and "Showing saved content" had sat in the layout as a bare `role="status"` paragraph since D282 and outlived the outage because the page was cached. Older podcasts shown as current are worse than an honest empty screen (owner, 13 Nov). Nothing checked where status text lives; `scripts/houseRules.test.ts` now fails on any `role="status"` or `role="alert"` element that is not on its list.
- Consequences: a page the worker kept before the change stays older until the worker's copy is replaced (10 minutes for a move, an hour for a kept-ready page), and the edge may serve its cached copy of a page for its stale window (D318). The player's error is a strip, so it needs the design system release that raises the strip (DS PR 73) and an app bump. Seven tests changed with his OK. The "controls did not load" spinner fallback, the plain-text 503 and the voice list's offline sentence stay as listed debt.
- Revisit if: a failure needs a second line on the strip, or a state appears that none of the four homes fits.
