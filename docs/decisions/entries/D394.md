## D394: Every state the reader tells is a strip, an empty screen, a toast or a field error

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13
- Decision: the strip is the one place for a one-line state, above everything including the open player (design system `--z-mini` + 1): offline, back online, "Showing older podcasts" (the page came from the saved copy, D282) and the player's line, one at a time in that order, words in `lib/stripMessages.ts`. A recording with no audio replaces the player's cover and controls with an empty screen (Open Downloads, Try again); the strip has no button, and a missing file is asked for again twice by itself first. The player's inline red notice, its portal and its scroll-into-view are removed. Shared failure sentences move to `lib/failureCopy.ts`. A saved-copy page is `no-store` at the edge.
- Why: during the Blob outage the player drew its own red block and a retry glyph on Play, a fifth way to say a state, and "Showing saved content" had sat in the layout as a bare `role="status"` paragraph since D282 and outlived the outage because the page was cached. Nothing checked where status text lives; `scripts/houseRules.test.ts` now fails on any `role="status"` or `role="alert"` element that is not on its list.
- Consequences: a page the worker saved during an outage keeps its saved-copy note until the worker's copy is replaced (10 minutes for a move, an hour for a kept-ready page). When both apply the player's line shows before the saved-copy note. the player's error is a strip, so it needs the design system release that raises the strip (DS PR 73) and an app bump. Seven tests changed with his OK. The "controls did not load" spinner fallback, the plain-text 503 and the voice list's offline sentence stay as listed debt.
- Revisit if: a failure needs a second line on the strip, or a state appears that none of the four homes fits.
