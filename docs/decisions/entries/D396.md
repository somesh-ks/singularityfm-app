## D396: Public podcasts: six read-only routes, audio from any secure host, a cookie switch

- Status: active
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decision: public shows come through six GET routes under `/api/podcasts/` (search, trending, show, new, cover, extras), built on Podcast Index with Apple's search as the fallback when the index fails or finds nothing. List answers are kept at Vercel's edge (search an hour, an Apple answer a day, trending six hours, a show and new episodes 15 minutes) with a day of stale-while-revalidate and a week of stale-if-error; failures are never kept. Each route answers a 308 to one canonical query first, so the edge holds one copy per question. A per-instance breaker skips the index for five minutes after five failures in a minute. The cover and extras routes fetch only addresses our own answers signed (a keyed hash from the index secret), through the source preview's outbound guard, and are kept a month at the edge, then revalidated, because a publisher can replace a file at the same address. The page's `media-src` adds `https:`. A cookie set by `/api/podcasts/preview?on` is the hidden switch; the slice that brings the new Home rewrites by it at the edge.
- Why: the Nov 12 plan and the Nov 23 architecture plan (owner's go, 23 Nov, "Now, in parallel"). Publishers route audio through tracking hops (up to six redirects measured on 12 Nov), so a host list cannot work; images and data requests stay at `'self'` because covers and transcripts come through our routes. Signing keeps the two file routes from being an open proxy, and a cookie lets the edge choose the page before paint, where a stored flag read by a script would swap the bars on screen.
- Consequences: the routes answer 503 until `PODCAST_INDEX_API_KEY` and `PODCAST_INDEX_API_SECRET` are on Vercel. The feed fallback for shows the index lacks waits for the slice that lets a feed address be pasted. Sponsor marks in the extras answer wait for slice 8, after the accuracy check. Rotating the index secret turns old cover addresses into 403s until the lists are fetched again.
- Revisit if: the index's lag on followed shows is worse than 15 minutes, edge hit rates leave function time above the Pro plan's included usage, or a host needs audio over http.
