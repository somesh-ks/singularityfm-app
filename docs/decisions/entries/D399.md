## D399: Claude steps fall back to Gemini Hydrogen before Sonnet; a cut-off or invalid reply skips the same model

- Status: amends D333, D334
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decision: every Claude-first step (story writer, rewrite, headline shorten, podcast script, opening and rewrite) runs Haiku medium, Haiku high, Gemini 4 Hydrogen (`gemini-hydrogen`, a new allowed family, id from config, effort medium sent as `thinkingLevel`), then Sonnet medium last (owner, 23 Nov: "stick to Haiku for cost, Gemini should kick in"). A reply cut off at the token limit (new kind `truncated`) or failing validation after its corrective retry (`invalid_output`) now skips the same model's later entries, as a model-wide failure already did (amends D333); only a refusal still tries the next effort. The fallback log carries `detail` (which rule failed, or the token limit) and `budget_left`. Gemini requests get the same thinking headroom as Claude. `MAX_TOKENS_PER_RUN` defaults to 500,000.
- Why: the 22 Nov weekly failed twice at the 400,000 cap. Haiku's replies were cut off at the token limit (the only `LlmOutputError` raised without a "reply failed validation" warning, and none appeared), Haiku high was cut off the same way, each attempt was billed in full, and Sonnet wrote the batch. The log said only `invalid_output`.
- Consequences: Hydrogen is priced at 1.50 and 7.50 USD per million tokens (third-party listings, unconfirmed), below Sonnet; a fallback costs less than before. Its id is not version-numbered, so the resolver uses the config id; a wrong id answers 404 and the chain moves to Sonnet. Amends D334's chain and the 2026-10-21 family list.
- Revisit if: Hydrogen serves more than one writer batch in five over two scheduled runs (Haiku's allowance or effort is then the fix), or Google renames the model.
