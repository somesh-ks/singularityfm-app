## D400: PostHog (EU) counts everyone cookieless; replay and a stored id only on opt-in

- Status: amends D154
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decision: PostHog's EU cloud is the one tool for product analytics, session replay, heatmaps, feature flags and A/B tests (owner, 23 Nov, from a review of Simple Analytics, Vercel and Cloudflare Web Analytics, GrowthBook, Statsig and Vercel Flags). Every counted reader (the production host, no Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, as D154) is counted cookieless: no cookie, no storage, no identifier; PostHog hashes the visit with a daily salt. "Help improve Singularity" on `/settings/privacy`, off by default, opts a reader in to a stored random id, replay with inputs masked and heatmaps (owner, 23 Nov: opt-in only, no consent banner). posthog-js is pinned and loaded at idle; it talks to `/api/ph`, rewritten to PostHog's EU hosts in `vercel.json`, so the page policy names no new host and ad blockers do not drop it. Simple Analytics runs alongside for four weeks, then goes if the counts agree. Engine switches stay in Neon (D249); reader-facing flags and A/B tests wait for sign-in and about 1,000 weekly readers.
- Why: the research's validation plan (source taps per session, the correction loop, weekly return, listen versus read) needs a session, and three of its five tests need a person across days, which no cookieless tool can give. An opt-in keeps D154's no-banner promise and still gives replays of the interview testers. At our volume PostHog's free allowance (1M events, 5,000 replays, 1M flag checks a month) covers it.
- Rejected: Cloudflare Web Analytics (no custom events), Vercel Web Analytics (same blind spots, metered), Vercel Flags (no Astro SDK), Statsig (bought by OpenAI), GrowthBook on Neon (a second tool plus our own event pipeline), a consent banner for everyone.
- Consequences: the Privacy page names PostHog and the switch; `mute` (a source blocked) and `read_end` (a briefing read to its end) join the events; `vote` down stands in for a correction. A "caught up" view does not exist yet, so the closure test waits for it. PostHog's project token is public and lives in `lib/analytics.ts`; empty, nothing loads.
- Revisit if: PostHog's free allowance is passed, its cookieless mode changes, or sign-in (phase 3) gives a consent moment that replaces the opt-in.
