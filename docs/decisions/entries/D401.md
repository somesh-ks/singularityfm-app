## D401: The bar is Home, Search and Library; Settings lives in Profile

- Status: amends D51 and D335
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Decision: the phone bar and the side column hold three destinations, Home (`/`), Search (`/search`) and Library (owner, 26 Nov: "the bottom menus should be: Home, Search, Libary. settings should be inside profile"). Settings, About, Privacy and Change log open from `/profile`, under the avatar, and their pages go back to Profile; on them no tab is lit. Search is a discover screen before typing (owner, 26 Nov; the 12 Nov canvas): what you opened from results lately, topic tiles with covers from each topic's chart, and the top shows; from two letters it lists Shows, Episodes and the Catchups the page holds. A topic opens `/topic?name=`, its top shows and what is new this week, under the Search tab. Topic tiles stop at 10rem wide from 40rem (design system), so a desktop column shows small tiles.
- Why: Settings is visited rarely and Search, with public podcasts, is a daily act; the bar should carry what a listener does every day. The 12 Nov canvas drew this bar.
- Rejected: a fourth tab for Settings (crowds the phone bar); a Search page that is only a field (owner: "use the real estate").
- Consequences: until the new Home ships the search bar at its top from 64rem, the side column keeps Search as a row. Favorites stays in Profile until the Library's Favorites chip. Search results for episodes come from the best matching show's newest episodes, since the index cannot search every episode.
- Revisit if: the slice 5 Home moves Search to its top bar on desktop, or sign-in puts account rows in Profile that crowd Settings.
