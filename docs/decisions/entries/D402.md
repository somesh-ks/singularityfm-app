- Status: amends D401
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Decision: `/` is the podcast Home (speed dial with filter chips, Continue listening, Catchups band, shelves). Today's briefing moves to `/latest`, a page under Home: its Back goes to `/`, and the bar marks Home while it is open. The Daily tile on the dial plays today's episode; the page opens from its Library row or a link (owner, 26 Nov, by card "Home at /, Latest at /latest"). - Why: the 12 Nov canvas puts Home first and the briefing inside it; keeping the briefing at `/` would have made Home a second front page.
- Consequences: about 100 browser tests moved from `/` to `/latest` (tests-ok on the PR). Library takes Favorites and Shows chips, so Favorites leave Profile. Hold menus on dial tiles and Continue cards share `cardHold.ts` with the episode cards.
