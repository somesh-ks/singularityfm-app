## D403: A podcast page is a list of headlines with times; shows and their episodes get pages and the player's rules

- Status: amends D77, D161, D343
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Decision: a Singularity podcast page (a daily and a Catchup alike) drops the section tabs and the Play chip on each story (owner, 26 Nov, by card: "C: grouped, tap opens summary", then "OK, Catchup pages follow too"). A story is its start time, its headline and its publisher on one row; the time is a text button that plays from there, and the headline opens the summary, "Read at <publisher>" and the votes in place. Sections stay as headings. Where the tabs stuck, a strip with the cover and the podcast's name sticks, and a press on it goes to the top. Play stays the floating button. The contents list beside the column on a wide screen stays. A public show has a page (`/show?id=`) and each of its episodes one (`/episode?id=`), static shells that read the id and ask the D396 routes. The device store (IndexedDB) holds favorites, positions and what was heard. One set of rules, in `podcasts/rules.ts`, connects them (flow spec, 23 Nov): a show episode is played when it ends or is left inside its last twentieth, three minutes at most; "New" is what was published after the show was favorited, plus the newest then; skips are 15 back and 30 on in a show; speed is kept per show, all Catchups counting as one. After a show episode, Up next offers the same show's next episodes, then one from each other favorite show, then the two newest Catchups; Autoplay never leaves the show (owner, 26 Nov: "use this space to suggest better and more").
- Why: the owner found the tabs "annoying" and the per-story Play noise; a list of headlines with times reads like chapters and shows more stories a screen. A `<details>` opens a summary without script, so the page reads with JavaScript off. One rules file keeps the show page, the dial, the player and Home from disagreeing on what is played or new.
- Rejected: A (a flat list, no sections) and B (summaries always open) from the three mock-ups; keeping the tabs on Catchup pages only; a row of Play chips; Autoplay crossing into another show; a server-side store before sign-in (phase 3).
- Consequences: the headline no longer links to the source (D77): the link is inside the opened story. The publisher follows the headline (D161). About 40 browser tests of the tabs and the chips were edited or deleted with his OK. A show episode has no transcript tab, no chapters and no Download yet. A pause inside the last twentieth marks the episode played.
- Revisit if: readers miss a way to jump between sections on a phone, or the tail rule marks half-heard episodes played.
