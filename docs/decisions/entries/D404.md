## D404: A public show's chapters, transcript and sponsor breaks, found by rules and trusted only on the transcript's own file

- Status: amends D403
- Date: 2026-11-27
- Decision: slice 8 of public podcasts. A show episode's publisher chapters become the player's timeline (Chapters tab, marks, previous and next by chapter) and its timed transcript the Transcript tab, one turn per speaker up to 45 s. Sponsor breaks come from the publisher's sponsor chapters and from the transcript by plain rules, no model: an opener phrase to the first address that names the sponsor, at most 150 s. The player trusts them only when the measured file matches the feed's length and the transcript ends inside it. The choice is a device preference: a Skip sponsor button with the time left (default), skip where found, or off; the episode page's switch sets the same choice. Decided for the owner overnight (26 to 27 Nov, AFK mode): the tab name Transcript, the default, the thresholds, no Undo on an automatic skip (a toast with an action stays until used), and that Download waits for its own PR.
- Why: measured on 19 real episodes of four shows, the rules marked 17 breaks, none running into the show, and missed 2. A host that inserts ads per listener (Darknet Diaries) serves a longer file than its transcript, so every time after an insert is late: the length check is what keeps a skip from cutting the show. Rules cost nothing per episode and can be read and tested.
- Rejected: a model to find breaks (a cost per episode and unreadable failures); stretching a break to the next address of any kind (it skipped two guests' introductions in a Latent Space episode); auto-skipping a break the listener sought into.
- Consequences: an ad read with no opener line or no address within 150 s is not marked. Breaks found in a transcript are not marked on a file with inserted ads; an insert that leaves the file within 5 s of the feed's length would still skip the wrong span. Download for show episodes needs the service worker to fetch publishers' audio, a CSP change of its own.
- Revisit if: he hears a skip land in the show, or misses breaks often on a show he plays.
